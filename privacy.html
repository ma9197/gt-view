GlassTube privacy

GlassTube is a personal app for one person's own glasses. It is not offered to the public.

  • It asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account, only to show your subscriptions, playlists and liked videos.
  • Your sign-in and settings stay on your device. There is no GlassTube server.
  • The app talks only to Google/YouTube, and to SponsorBlock (it sends the video ID to find sponsor segments).
  • Nothing is sold or shared. Remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
